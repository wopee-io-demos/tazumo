# Design: commerce-rules

## Shape
`priceBreakdown(market: MarketConfig, order: OrderInput): PriceBreakdown`. `getMarket(code)` resolves the country codes `SK`, `CZ`, `DE` and `AT` and throws `unknown_market` otherwise. Every amount is integer minor units of the market currency; arithmetic uses `BigInt` and overflows are rejected. At most 100 lines and 999 units per line.

Order lines carry `id`, `quantity`, `unitPrice` (`Money`), `vatCategory` and an optional `vatRateId`, which must be the category default or one of the category's allowed overrides (`categoryOverrides`, for example AT `groceries` → `basicFood` 4.9 %). `paymentMethod` is `card` (default, never rounded) or `cash` (market cash rounding, CZK whole crowns). The result lists `components` in display order and always ends with `payable`. Components with `addsToPayable` sum to `payableMinor`; VAT contained in VAT-inclusive prices is shown with `addsToPayable: false`.

Rationale, rate table, sources and assumptions: [ADR-0018](../../../docs/adr/0018-commerce-rules.md).

## How apps/api will consume it (follow-up, API owner)
1. Add `"@tazumo/commerce": "workspace:*"` to `apps/api` dependencies.
2. Implement the existing contract operation `POST /v1/order-quotes` (`QuoteRequest`: `marketCode`, `merchantId`, `items[{ productId, quantity }]`, `deliveryAddress`); do not add a parallel endpoint. The client never sends prices or VAT categories: the route loads `price_minor`, `currency`, the product VAT category and any allowed rate override from PostgreSQL for the merchant's products.
3. Resolve the commerce market from the market row's `countryCode`, not from `Market.code` (a city slug such as `prague`). The only seeded market today, `demo-eu` (IE), has no commerce configuration: until an IE market is added or the seed moves to a launch country, the API turns the package's `unknown_market` into 422 `unsupported_market` whenever the market row exists but its `countryCode` has no commerce configuration, rather than guessing taxes.
4. Distance comes from a server-side geocoding or zone lookup on `deliveryAddress` (PostGIS per ADR-0005). Until that exists the fee stays synthetic and is labelled so.
5. Call `priceBreakdown(getMarket(countryCode), order)` and extend `Quote` additively: keep `subtotal`, `deliveryFee`, `tax` (sum of VAT groups; contained in the total when `pricesIncludeVat`) and keep `total` = `totalMinor`, so `total` always equals the VAT-group gross amounts it contains. Expose the cash rounding separately as optional `rounding` and `payable` (they differ from `total` only for CZK cash payments; card payments are not rounded). Add optional `smallOrderSurcharge`, `paymentMethod`, `pricesIncludeVat`, `vat[]`, `components[]` (`kind`, `amount`, `addsToPayable`, `synthetic`, `explanation`) and `dataStatus`, so clients can show the synthetic and re-verify notice. Contract `OrderItem.quantity` is capped at 100, within the package limit of 999; add `maxItems: 100` to `QuoteRequest.items` to match `MAX_LINES`.
6. Error mapping: a market row, merchant or product missing from the database → 404; `invalid_quantity`, `invalid_distance`, `duplicate_line`, `empty_order` and `too_many_lines` → 400; `outside_delivery_area` and `unknown_market` for an existing market row (as `unsupported_market`) → 422. Because the server builds the lines from its own data, `invalid_line`, `invalid_amount`, `currency_mismatch`, `unknown_vat_category`, `unknown_vat_rate`, `invalid_market_config` and `amount_overflow` indicate bad server data → 500, logged without customer data.
7. Products need a VAT category column; the catalogue category (`Food`, `Groceries`, `Shops`, `Gifts`) is not a tax classification.
