# Change: Preliminary brand-name screening (not legal clearance)

Issue: [#6](https://github.com/wopee-io-demos/tazumo/issues/6)

## Why
The shortlisted names Tazumo, Zupomi and Zopiku have never been screened. The provisional name ships in the apps. A reproducible record of what public sources show today lets a human decide whether to commission a formal trademark search, and for which name.

## What changes
- `docs/brand/NAME-SCREENING.md` records, for each name: the trademark database queries (EUIPO eSearch plus, WIPO Global Brand Database, TMview) with date, strategy, result count and near matches in Nice classes 9, 35, 39 and 43; DNS, WHOIS and RDAP observations for .com, .eu, .cz, .sk and .de; social-handle observations; and pronunciation and meaning notes for EN, CS, SK and DE.
- It links the "accessible brand tokens" and "original icon and mascot" criteria to PR #44 evidence.

## Out of scope
- Formal clearance, legal opinions, registrability or freedom-to-operate statements. Those need a trademark attorney, arranged by a human.
- Registering domains or handles, or renaming anything in code. Name stays independent of palette and mascot (ADR-0002).
