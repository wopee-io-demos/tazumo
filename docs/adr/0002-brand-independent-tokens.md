# ADR-0002: Independent brand identity and palette

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-20

## Context
The shortlisted names Tazumo, Zupomi and Zopiku, and their palettes and fox mascot directions were evaluated independently. Legal clearance is incomplete.

## Decision
Represent brand naming, palette and mascot concept as separate values. Provide alternate color tokens, including violet, coral, emerald and midnight. Keep the shipped name provisional.

## Consequences
Brand changes do not rewrite domain models, merchant data or payment configuration. Mascot emoji is a temporary concept only. Color accessibility and an original trademark-safe visual identity are launch blockers.
